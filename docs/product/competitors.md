## Lessons from others

- Success is partial: Mine reported 64% completion early on, and tech companies were least responsive. **Follow-up and escalation matter.**
- Deletion can be "soft". **Verification matters.**

## Our differentiators

1. DPDP-native (rights, deadlines, retention overrides, DPB escalation).
2. Discovery from email **and** SMS/OTP **and** Sign in with Google.
3. Lives inside existing apps via MCP; no new app to install.
4. Daily prevention, not one-time cleanup.
5. Tiered requests (opt-out → withdraw → restrict → erase), not delete-or-nothing.
6. Approval tiers and a tamper-evident audit log.
7. Hindi and regional languages.
8. Built to plug into Consent Managers once they exist.
